## Purpose

A person browsing the public site without being signed in.

## Goals

- See which coins Royal Aurum offers and what makes each one distinctive.
- Look at coins that were sold in the past.
- Offer a coin to Royal Aurum, get in touch, or sign up for news of new acquisitions.

## Responsibilities

Provides accurate details in any form they submit.

## Boundaries

Cannot see unpublished coins or any administration function. Whether a visitor may buy
without creating an account is not yet decided (question Q-C6).
