## Definition

A single, unique, physical certified gold coin offered for sale. It has a stock of one.

## Distinguish From

Not a product with a quantity or variants. A coin offered by a private seller is the subject of a Listing (TERM-LISTING).

## Usage

Shown on coin cards, coin pages, the cart, orders and the Sold Archive.
