## Definition

A person who buys, or may buy, coins on the site. A customer with a registered account has an account area, an order history and a Wishlist. Whether buying requires an account is not yet decided (question Q-C6).

## Distinguish From

Not a seller acting in that capacity and not an administrator.

## Usage

Account area, order history, Wishlist, checkout.
