## Definition

The holding of a coin for one buyer for a limited period, during which nobody else can buy it. It applies while a bank transfer is awaited and, in Phase 2, after an offer has been accepted.

## Distinguish From

Not a sale: a reservation that runs out releases the coin. Not the Wishlist, which holds nothing.

## Usage

Bank-transfer checkout (period configurable by the Client) and accepted offers (48 hours).
