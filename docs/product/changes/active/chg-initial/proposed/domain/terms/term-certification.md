## Definition

Independent grading and encapsulation of a coin by a certification service, identified by the service, the certification number and the grade. For Marketplace listings only PCGS, NGC and CAC Grading are accepted.

## Distinguish From

Not an authentication, valuation or grading performed by Royal Aurum or by the Platform, which are excluded from the Services.

## Usage

Shown on every coin page; mandatory on every Marketplace listing.
