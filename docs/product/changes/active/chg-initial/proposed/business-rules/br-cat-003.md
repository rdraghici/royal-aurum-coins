## Rule

The BUY section is organised into categories. The initial categories are US Pre-1933 Gold,
British Gold and European Gold. The Client can add further categories and manages categories
in the administration panel.

## Rationale

These three groups are what Royal Aurum primarily sells; the Client must be able to extend them alone.

## Examples

The Client adds a category "World Gold" in the administration panel and assigns coins to it.

## Exceptions

Whether a coin can belong to more than one category has not been discussed; exactly one is assumed.
