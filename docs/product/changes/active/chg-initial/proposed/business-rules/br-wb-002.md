## Rule

A We Buy submission has one of these statuses: new, under review, accepted, declined.

## Rationale

The Agreement names these four as examples; they are proposed here as the complete set.

## Examples

A new submission is opened by the Client and set to under review, later to declined.

## Exceptions

None.
