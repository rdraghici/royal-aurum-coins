## Rule

The BUY section can be filtered by category, grade, certification service and price, and
sorted by price and by date added.

## Rationale

Proposed by the Provider; the exact set is the Provider's to settle (question Q-R6).

## Examples

A visitor filters British Gold to coins graded by NGC and sorts them by price, lowest first.

## Exceptions

None proposed.
