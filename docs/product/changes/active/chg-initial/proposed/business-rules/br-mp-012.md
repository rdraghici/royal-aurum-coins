## Rule

The Client has an overview of all Marketplace listings, orders, payment statuses, payouts and
commissions, and can suspend sellers or listings. Notification emails are sent to sellers,
buyers and the Client at each key step: submission, approval or rejection, sale, shipment,
delivery confirmation, payout and refund.

## Rationale

The Client answers for the Marketplace and must see and be able to stop everything on it.

## Examples

The Client suspends a seller; a listing is approved and its seller is emailed; a payout is made and the seller and the Client are emailed.

## Exceptions

What suspension does to a seller's live listings and open orders has not been discussed.
