## Purpose

A person acting for the Client (Royal Aurum) in the administration panel. The Client
operates the Platform alone, without technical assistance.

## Goals

- Add, edit, publish, sell and archive coins and choose what the homepage features.
- Handle orders, customers, We Buy submissions and subscribers.
- In Phase 2: approve or reject listings, set commission, cancel and refund, suspend
  sellers or listings.

## Responsibilities

Decides what is published and approved; remains solely responsible for the accuracy of
published information and for approving each listing.

## Boundaries

Does not hold Marketplace customer funds: those are held by the Payment Provider.
