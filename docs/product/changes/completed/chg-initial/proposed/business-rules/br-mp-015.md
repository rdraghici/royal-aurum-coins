## Rule

A listing has one of these statuses: draft, in review, approved, live, sold, rejected.

## Rationale

The Agreement speaks of listing statuses without naming them (question Q-R3).

## Examples

A seller saves a draft, submits it (in review), and the Client approves it (approved, then live).

## Exceptions

Withdrawn and suspended listings are not covered by this proposed set.
