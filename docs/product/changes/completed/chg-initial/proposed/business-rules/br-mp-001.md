## Rule

Only gold coins may be listed on the Marketplace.

## Rationale

Royal Aurum is a specialist in historical gold coins; the Marketplace keeps that focus.

## Examples

A seller cannot create a listing for a silver coin or for a gold bar.

## Exceptions

None. The technical rule assists but does not replace the Client's own review of each listing.
