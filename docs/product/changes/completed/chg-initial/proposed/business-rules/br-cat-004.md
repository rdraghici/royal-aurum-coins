## Rule

The Sold Archive displays previously sold coins with a "Sold" label and with purchasing
disabled. A sold coin is movable to the Sold Archive.

## Rationale

Past sales show the quality of what Royal Aurum handles, without suggesting the coin is still available.

## Examples

The 1794 George III Guinea was sold last month. It appears in the Sold Archive marked "Sold";
its page offers no way to add it to a cart.

## Exceptions

Not yet decided: whether a sold coin appears in the Sold Archive automatically or only when
the Client moves it there (question Q-C31); whether the price it sold for is shown (question
Q-C12); whether Marketplace coins appear in it.
