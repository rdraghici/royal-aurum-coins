## Rule

A person can offer a coin directly to Royal Aurum through a submission form that includes coin
details, certification details and photo uploads. Each submission is stored in the
administration panel with a status. The Client receives an email notification and the
submitter receives an automatic confirmation. The Client decides at its discretion whether to
purchase.

## Rationale

Royal Aurum sources coins from private owners; the form gives it everything needed to decide.

## Examples

An owner submits a certified sovereign with its certification number and four photographs. Royal Aurum is notified and the owner receives a confirmation.

## Exceptions

Not yet decided: whether an account is required to submit, the exact fields, and the limits on
photo uploads. Any purchase that follows is settled outside the Platform.
