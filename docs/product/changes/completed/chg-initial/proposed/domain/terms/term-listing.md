## Definition

A seller's coin offered on the Marketplace. It becomes visible on the Platform only after the Client has approved it.

## Distinguish From

Not one of Royal Aurum's own coins, from which it must be clearly distinguishable, and not a We Buy submission.

## Usage

Listing form, approval queue, seller dashboard, BUY section once approved.
