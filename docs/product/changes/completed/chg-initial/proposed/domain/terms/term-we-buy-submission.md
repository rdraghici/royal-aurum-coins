## Definition

An offer of a coin made directly to Royal Aurum through the We Buy form, with coin details, certification details and photographs. The Client decides at its discretion whether to purchase.

## Distinguish From

Not a Marketplace listing: nothing is published and no sale takes place on the Platform.

## Usage

The SELL area; stored in the administration panel with a status.
