## Requirement

The Platform MUST provide: a homepage based on the Approved Design Direction, including the
hero section and the featured selection; a SELL area containing the We Buy section and the
entry point to the Marketplace; and the content pages About, Contact (with a contact form),
FAQ, Shipping, Returns, Terms, Privacy and Cookies, populated with texts supplied by the
Client.

## Rationale

These pages are the agreed public structure of the site; their texts are the Client's.
