## Definition

A person who buys, or may buy, coins on the site, with or without a registered account. A
customer with a registered account has an account area, an order history and a Wishlist. A
guest buyer checks out with an email address and a shipping address and is offered an account
afterwards.

## Distinguish From

Not a seller acting in that capacity and not an administrator.

## Usage

Account area, order history, Wishlist, checkout.
