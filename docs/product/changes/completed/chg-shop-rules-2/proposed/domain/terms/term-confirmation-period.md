## Definition

The period, configurable by the Client and set at 7 calendar days, that starts when delivery of
a Marketplace order is confirmed. During it the buyer can report a problem with the coin or
request a return. When it ends with no open problem report, and unless the Client has cancelled
the order, the held payment is released automatically to the seller.

## Distinguish From

Not the bank-transfer reservation period and not the validity of an offer. Not counted from
shipment.

## Usage

Marketplace orders (Phase 2): starts at confirmed delivery (BR-MP-016), governs problem
reports (BR-MP-017) and the automatic payout (BR-MP-006).
