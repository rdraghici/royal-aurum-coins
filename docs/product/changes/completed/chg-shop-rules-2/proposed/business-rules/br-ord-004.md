## Rule

Card and bank transfer are both offered for every coin, whatever its price; there is no price
threshold. At the choice of payment method, bank transfer is presented as the recommended
option for higher-value purchases. Neither method is ever mandatory.

## Rationale

The Agreement makes bank transfer the preferred route for higher-value coins; the Client
decided on 15 Oct 2026 that this is a recommendation shown to the buyer, not a rule tied to a
price, so that no buyer is refused a card payment.

## Examples

A buyer of a EUR 5,950 coin sees both methods, with a note that bank transfer is recommended
for higher-value purchases, and may still pay by card.

## Exceptions

None.
