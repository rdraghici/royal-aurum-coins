## Rule

The homepage has a section of featured coins. Which coins appear in it is selected by the
Client from the administration panel.

## Rationale

The homepage must present a small, deliberate selection, in keeping with the curated positioning.

## Examples

The Client selects three coins; the homepage shows exactly those three under "Selected Coins".

## Exceptions

What the section shows when a featured coin has been sold or unpublished has not been discussed.
