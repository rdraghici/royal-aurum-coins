## Rule

Only coins certified by PCGS, NGC or CAC Grading may be listed on the Marketplace.

## Rationale

Certification by a recognised service is the basis of trust between strangers; the Platform does not authenticate coins.

## Examples

A listing naming another grading service, or none, cannot be submitted.

## Exceptions

None.
