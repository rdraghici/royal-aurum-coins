## Rule

The Marketplace is separate from the We Buy section, in which a seller offers a coin directly
to Royal Aurum.

## Rationale

They are different transactions: on the Marketplace a third party buys; in We Buy, Royal Aurum may buy.

## Examples

A We Buy submission never appears in the approval queue and never becomes a listing by itself.

## Exceptions

None.
