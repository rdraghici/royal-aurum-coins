## Rule

Every coin carries: title, category, price, certification service, certification number,
grade, gold content, weight, year, mint, a detailed description and photographs. The coin page
presents a high-resolution image gallery with zoom together with these details, generated from
the structured record so that all coin pages are consistent.

## Rationale

Buyers of certified coins compare exactly these facts; structured data keeps every page uniform.

## Examples

Title "1908 $20 Saint-Gaudens", category US Pre-1933 Gold, certification service PCGS, grade
MS65, a certification number, gold content, weight, year 1908, a mint, a description and
photographs. (Illustrative values from the mock-up, not real inventory.)

## Exceptions

Not yet decided: the units in which gold content and weight are recorded and shown, and
whether denomination is a separate field (questions Q-R14 and Q-C32).
