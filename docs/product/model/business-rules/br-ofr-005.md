## Rule

There are no counter-offers, no bidding and no public display of offers. The administrator can
see all offers and every seller's private minimum.

## Rationale

Auction, bidding and price negotiation remain excluded from the scope; the Client must be able to oversee offers.

## Examples

A seller can only accept or decline; there is no field for proposing a different price. Nobody browsing a coin can see that offers exist on it.

## Exceptions

None.
