## Rule

The Client can add, edit, publish, unpublish, mark as sold and archive coins in the
administration panel. A coin that is not published is not shown on the public site.

## Rationale

The Client prepares a coin (photos, description, price) before showing it, and can withdraw it at any time.

## Examples

A coin saved without being published is visible to administrators only. After the Client publishes it, it appears in its category.

## Exceptions

None.
