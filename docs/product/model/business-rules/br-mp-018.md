## Rule

For each listing the seller sets the shipping charge and the insurance arrangement for the
shipment. Both are shown to the buyer before payment, and the shipping charge is part of the
amount the buyer pays.

## Rationale

Marketplace coins are shipped by their sellers, not by Royal Aurum, so the cost and the cover
are the seller's to decide and the buyer's to see before committing.

## Examples

A seller lists a coin at EUR 3,000 with a shipping charge of EUR 25, insured. The listing and
the checkout show EUR 3,000, the EUR 25 charge and the insurance before the buyer pays.

## Exceptions

Whether the commission applies to the shipping charge or only to the coin price is not decided
(question Q-C39). Which countries a seller may ship to, and whether the shipping charge is part
of the information a listing must have before submission (BR-MP-003), have not been discussed.
