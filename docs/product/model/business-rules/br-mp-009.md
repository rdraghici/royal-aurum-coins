## Rule

Royal Aurum's own coins and Marketplace coins are clearly distinguishable on the Platform, in
the manner agreed in the final design. The identity of individual sellers is not shown to
buyers unless the Client instructs otherwise.

## Rationale

Buyers must know whose coin they are buying, without the Marketplace turning into a directory of sellers.

## Examples

A Marketplace coin carries a label such as "Offered by a Royal Aurum seller" (the Provider's suggested wording, not yet agreed); no seller name appears.

## Exceptions

The manner of distinction is still to be agreed in the final design (questions Q-C23 and Q-A5).
