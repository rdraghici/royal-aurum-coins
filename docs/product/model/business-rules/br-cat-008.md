## Rule

The certification service of a Royal Aurum coin is one of PCGS, NGC or CAC Grading, the same
three services that are accepted for Marketplace listings.

## Rationale

Never discussed for Royal Aurum's own coins; the Agreement fixes the three services for the Marketplace only.

## Examples

The Client adds a coin and chooses NGC from the three services.

## Exceptions

If Royal Aurum also sells coins certified by another service, this rule must list it.
