## Purpose

A person who buys coins on the site; also called the buyer. Buying does not require an
account: a guest can check out and is offered an account afterwards. A customer who is
signed in has an account area and a Wishlist.

## Goals

- Buy a coin by card or by bank transfer.
- Follow their orders and keep their account details up to date.
- Keep a Wishlist of coins they are interested in.

## Responsibilities

Pays for what they order within the time allowed. In Phase 2, confirms delivery of a
Marketplace purchase.

## Boundaries

Sees only their own orders and data. Has no access to the administration panel. Does not
learn the identity of a Marketplace seller unless the Client instructs otherwise.
