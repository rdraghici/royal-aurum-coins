## Purpose

Phase 2. A private person who lists certified gold coins on the Marketplace.

## Goals

- List a coin, have it approved and sell it.
- Be paid automatically after delivery, net of the commission.

## Responsibilities

Completes the Payment Provider's identity verification and payout onboarding, provides
complete and accurate listing information, ships the sold coin and records tracking.

## Boundaries

A listing is not visible until the Client approves it. The seller's identity is not shown
to buyers unless the Client instructs otherwise. A seller offering a coin directly to
Royal Aurum uses We Buy instead, which is separate.
