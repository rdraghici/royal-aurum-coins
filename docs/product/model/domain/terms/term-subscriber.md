## Definition

A person who has signed up for new-acquisitions emails, given consent and confirmed their address through double opt-in.

## Distinguish From

Not a customer account: subscribing does not create an account, and having an account does not subscribe anyone.

## Usage

Newsletter signup form; subscriber management and export in the administration panel.
