## Definition

A buyer's statement, made during the confirmation period of a Marketplace order, that the coin
received is not authentic, is damaged or is not as described, or the buyer's request to return
it. While it is open it holds the payout; the Client closes it with a refund or a release.

## Distinguish From

Not a message to the seller: there is no messaging between buyers and sellers. Not a dispute
procedure: the Client alone decides. Not a cancellation before shipment.

## Usage

Raised from the buyer's order during the confirmation period; handled by the Client in the
administration panel; the Client is notified when one is raised.
