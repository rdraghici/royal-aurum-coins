## Definition

A grouping of coins in the BUY section. The initial categories are US Pre-1933 Gold, British Gold and European Gold; the Client can add more.

## Distinguish From

Not a filter such as grade or price, and not a certification service.

## Usage

Used for navigating the BUY section and when the Client classifies a coin.
