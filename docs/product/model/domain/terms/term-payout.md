## Definition

The automatic release of held funds to the seller, net of the Client's commission. In the fallback of Clause 7.9 (no Payment Provider approves held payments) payouts are instead executed manually by the Client after delivery.

## Distinguish From

Not a refund, which returns the payment to the buyer before release.

## Usage

Triggered by the buyer's confirmation of delivery or by the end of the confirmation period.
