## Definition

The grade assigned to a coin by its certification service and printed on the holder, for example MS65 or AU55.

## Distinguish From

Not a quality score given by Royal Aurum, a seller or a buyer.

## Usage

Displayed with the certification service and number; proposed as a catalogue filter.
