## Definition

Stripe (Stripe Connect) or, where applicable under Clause 7 of the Agreement, an Alternative Payment Provider.

## Distinguish From

Not the Client's bank, to which bank transfers for Royal Aurum's own coins are paid directly.

## Usage

Card payments; in Phase 2 held funds, payouts, refunds and seller verification.
