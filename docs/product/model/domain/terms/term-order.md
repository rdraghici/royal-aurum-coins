## Definition

A buyer's purchase of a coin through the site, with a status the customer and the Client can follow.

## Distinguish From

Not a We Buy submission and not an offer. A Marketplace order additionally involves held funds and a payout.

## Usage

Created at checkout; listed in the customer's order history and in the administration panel.
