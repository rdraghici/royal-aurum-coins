## Definition

The way VAT applies to the sale of a particular coin, chosen by the Client per coin from a list
the Client maintains. The initial treatments are the investment-gold exemption, the margin
scheme and standard VAT. A treatment explains a coin's final price; it never changes it.

## Distinguish From

Not a tax added at checkout: no VAT is ever added to a displayed price. Not a shipping zone or
a tax-zone setting.

## Usage

Set on each coin in the administration panel; shown on the coin page; recorded on the order as
it stood when the order was placed; shown on the order confirmation.
