## Definition

A buyer's payment for a Marketplace purchase while it is held by the Payment Provider, and not by the Client, before release.

## Distinguish From

Not money in the Client's bank account: the Client at no time holds Marketplace customer funds in its own bank account.

## Usage

Between the buyer's payment and the release to the seller or the refund to the buyer.
