## Definition

The moment a purchase counts as made, at which the coin is marked as sold: for a card payment, when the payment succeeds; for a bank transfer, when the payment has been received.

## Distinguish From

Not the placing of a bank-transfer order, which only reserves the coin.

## Usage

Decides when a coin changes from reserved or available to sold.
