## Definition

A period configurable by the Client after which, unless the Client has cancelled the order, the held payment is released automatically even if the buyer has not confirmed delivery.

## Distinguish From

Not the bank-transfer reservation period and not the validity of an offer.

## Usage

Marketplace orders. What starts the period is not yet decided (questions Q-C22 and Q-R4).
