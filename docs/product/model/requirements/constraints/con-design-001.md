## Constraint

The Platform follows the Approved Design Direction, retaining its clean, light and premium
overall design and the large homepage headline "Invest in Gold. Own History." The homepage
hero features a certified coin as the main focus against a subtle, slightly out-of-focus
historical background (for example an old gold coin reference book or a historical document).
The overall look communicates within a few seconds that Royal Aurum offers carefully selected,
high-quality historical gold coins, and avoids the appearance of a traditional coin shop with
excessive information or hundreds of products on screen.

## Rationale

The Client chose this direction on 5 October 2026; a departure from it is a Change Request (Clause 5.3).

## Consequences

- Pages show few coins with generous space rather than dense grids.
- Mock-up images and texts are placeholders; real content comes from the Client.
